Replace the title and permalink with the proposal's number and topic.

## Summary

Describe the requested change and link the affected specification requirements.

## Motivation

Explain the problem, who encounters it, and the evidence that shows a specification change is needed.

## Specification text

### Before

Quote or link the current wording. Indicate where a new requirement would be added.

### After

Provide the proposed normative wording, with any new or changed requirement IDs.

## Compatibility

Describe which data, producers or consumers are affected. Include relevant versioning or deprecation implications and the conformance examples that would demonstrate the change.

## Comment window

Record the opening and closing UTC calendar dates and the thread for comments.

- Opens YYYY-MM-DD
- Closes YYYY-MM-DD

## Decision

Record the outcome and its UTC date. Explain the reasons and link the accepted wording or follow-up work. Retain the proposal and this section for every outcome.
